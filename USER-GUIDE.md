# Penumbra: quick guide

Penumbra puts a thin line of light around the edges of your screen that **moves with your music**. It takes its colors from the song's cover (or from the video you're watching), and it can show the **lyrics** in time with the song.

---

## 1. Install

1. Download **Penumbra-Setup-1.0.0.exe** from the **[download page](https://github.com/SadMan-ZaMaN/Penumbra-download/releases/latest)** (under **Assets**).
2. Run it. If Windows says **"Windows protected your PC"**, click **More info → Run anyway**. (The app isn't signed; that's all this means.)
3. Click through the installer. Penumbra starts and opens its settings panel.

**You need:** Windows 10 (version 2004 or newer) or Windows 11, 64-bit. Nothing else to install.

---

## 2. Start using it

- **Play some music:** YouTube Music, Spotify, YouTube in a browser… The edges light up while there's sound and fade away when it's quiet.
- **The tray icon** (a small ring next to the clock) is Penumbra. **Left-click** it for the settings panel, **right-click** for a quick menu. Can't see it? It may be hidden under the **^** arrow. Opening Penumbra from the Start menu again also shows the panel.
- **Lyrics:** in the panel, switch on **Show lyrics**.

---

## 3. Keyboard shortcuts

These work from anywhere, in any app.

| Keys | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Ctrl + Alt + P** | Light on / off (handy for games and video calls) |
| **Ctrl + Alt + N** | Opens or closes the full-screen **Now playing** view. During **Night sky**, it lifts the black screen and brings it back. |
| **Ctrl + Alt + M** | Shows or hides the **mini player**, a small bar to control the music without leaving your app. |

On the Now playing view, **Space** plays / pauses and **Esc** closes it.

---

## 4. Lyrics styles

Pick one under **Show lyrics** in the panel:

- **Flow:** one line above the taskbar, filling in with color as it's sung (like karaoke).
- **Constellation:** each word appears as a star, joined to the one before.
- **Night sky:** the stars on a fully black screen, with the light around the edges.
  - Press **Ctrl + Alt + N** to see your desktop again (and again to go back to black).
  - Your apps keep running underneath, and clicks still reach them.

**Lyrics too early or late?** Move the **Timing** slider (up to 4 s either way).
**Color filling too slowly or too fast?** Move the **Fill speed** slider (from 2× slower to 2× faster).
Both are just for the song playing: the next song starts on time and at normal speed again.

---

## 5. The Now playing view

A full-screen player: the song's cover on the left, the lyrics scrolling on the right.

- **Open it:** the **⤢** button at the top of the panel, or **Ctrl + Alt + N**.
- **Under the cover:** a progress bar (click or drag it to jump) and **previous / play-pause / next**.
- **Background (🎨 button, top right):** **Cover** (a blurred cover, the default), **Mine** (one color you pick) or **Tinted** (the cover washed with your color), with a brightness slider. This lasts for the current song.
- **Want to pick another song in YouTube Music?** Press **Ctrl + Alt + N**, choose your song, and press it again. The view comes back as you left it.
- **⚙** opens the settings panel on top of it.

**The mini player** is the small version: a bar that floats over your apps with the cover, the song, its lyric line filling in, and **previous / play-pause / next**.

- **Show or hide it:** **Ctrl + Alt + M**, the small-window button at the top of the settings panel (next to **⤢**; outlined while it's on), **Mini player** in the tray menu, or its **✕**.
- **Its other buttons:** **⚙** opens the settings panel (click again to close), **⤢** opens the Now playing view.
- **Move it:** drag it by any empty spot. **Double-click** it to send it back to the bottom middle. When you close it, it opens at the bottom middle again next time.
- **Tuck it away:** **›** slides it into the side of the screen, leaving a small tab with the cover. Click the tab to bring it back; drag the tab to move it.
- Clicking it doesn't take the keyboard away from what you're typing in.
- While it shows, Flow's own line at the bottom of the screen hides (the bar shows the same line).

## 6. The settings panel

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Switch at the top** | Light on / off |
| **Colors** | **Auto** (cover for music, screen for videos), **Album art**, **Screen**, or **Mine** (pick two colors or a preset) |
| **Thickness / Glow / Brightness / Waviness** | How thick, soft, bright and wavy the line is |
| **Corners** | Square, Rounded or Round |
| **Motion** | **Music sync** (reacts to the beat) or **Steady** (a calm light that stays on) |
| **Beats** | **Calm**, **Normal** or **Lively**: how strongly the light reacts to the beat |
| **Smoothness** | **Smooth**, **Balanced** or **Saver** (saves battery) |
| **Glow when nothing is playing** | A dim light even when it's quiet |
| **Show in screenshots & recordings** | Off by default, so the light stays out of screen shares |
| **OLED care** | Moves the light a few pixels very slowly, to protect OLED screens |
| **Start with Windows** | Starts Penumbra when you log in |
| **↻** (bottom) | Back to the default look (also switches lyrics off) |
| **⟳** (bottom) | Restarts Penumbra if anything seems stuck. Your settings stay. |
| **Quit Penumbra** | Closes it |

## 7. Something not right?

| Problem | Try this |
|---|---|
| No light | Is something playing **with sound**? Press **Ctrl + Alt + P** in case the light is off. |
| No lyrics | Some songs just don't have lyrics in the free lyrics database. The panel tells you. Private / Incognito browser windows hide the song name, so lyrics can't be found there. |
| "Only untimed lyrics for this song" | The words exist, but without timing. They show as a slowly scrolling list on the Now playing view. |
| Light stopped after switching to Bluetooth earbuds | It reconnects by itself within a few seconds of the music playing. If not, press **⟳** in the panel. |
| The light isn't in my screen recording | Turn on **Show in screenshots & recordings**. |
| Games feel slower | Turn the light off with **Ctrl + Alt + P** while gaming. |

---

## 8. Privacy

- Penumbra **listens to what your PC plays** to find the beat. It's never recorded or saved, and it never uses your microphone.
- **Lyrics:** only with **Show lyrics** on, the song's title and artist are sent to **lrclib.net** to find them.
- **Sharp covers:** only while the Now playing view is open, the title and artist are sent to **Apple's iTunes search**.
- Nothing else leaves your PC. The only file it saves is its settings.

---

## 9. Update or remove

- **Update:** download the newer Setup from the [download page](https://github.com/SadMan-ZaMaN/Penumbra-download/releases/latest) and run it. It installs over the old one and keeps your settings.
- **Remove:** Windows **Settings → Apps → Penumbra → Uninstall**.
